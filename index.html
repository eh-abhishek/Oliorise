Our Process

Carefully handled. Naturally inspired.

Our shared brand material describes a slow settling and filtration approach after the oil is prepared, with an emphasis on avoiding unnecessary processing.

01

Prepare

Oil is prepared with attention to the raw material and the desired traditional character.

02

Cold Press

The range is presented as cold-pressed oils, aligned with the brand's core identity.

03

Natural Settling

The supplied brand copy describes keeping prepared oil still for 24–48 hours so fine natural particles can settle.

04

Careful Filtration

After settling, the oil is carefully separated/filtered, aiming to retain its natural character.
